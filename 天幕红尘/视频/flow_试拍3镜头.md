# Google Flow 试拍：第01集 3 个关键镜头

用于和 Wan2.2 版本对比。Veo 每段约 8 秒，模式选 Text to Video，比例 16:9。
对白写在提示词里，测试 Veo 中文口型和发音。暴力内容只用声音和遮挡处理，降低被审核拦下的概率。

---

## 镜头 A：1-1 冷开场（巴黎餐厅台阶，夜）

```
Cinematic 35mm film, 1992, night in Paris. An elegant classical French restaurant entrance with four stone steps and warm door light on a quiet commercial street. A tall Chinese man in his late 30s, wearing a plain white crewneck sports hoodie and sneakers, walks calmly down the steps, takes a steel flip lighter from his pocket and flicks it open. A sudden muffled gunshot. He stops, looks down at a dark red stain spreading on the white hoodie, presses his hand to his chest, and says in Mandarin Chinese with a dry, mocking calm: "还真他妈抬举我。" Then he slowly sits down on the steps. Handheld camera, low-key lighting, muted colors, film grain, realistic. No subtitles, no text.
```

## 镜头 B：1-7 诀别电话（罗家明，莫斯科深夜）

```
Cinematic 35mm film, 1991, a dim Moscow office at midnight, a single warm desk lamp and cold blue light from the window. A 42-year-old Chinese businessman with short black hair, loosened tie and sweat-stained white shirt holds a black telephone receiver, eyes red, forcing a normal voice. He says softly in Mandarin Chinese: "我记着呢，妈……您心脏不好，往后不管遇到什么事，按时吃药……" His hand trembles, he wipes his eyes with his sleeve. Slow push-in close-up, shallow depth of field, film grain, realistic skin texture. Faint tinny elderly woman's voice from the receiver. No subtitles, no text.
```

## 镜头 C：1-8 横幅（见路不走）

```
Cinematic 35mm film, 1991, a dark Moscow office at night lit only by a desk lamp. On the wood-paneled wall hangs a framed Chinese calligraphy banner with four large brush characters "见路不走" in black ink on aged rice paper, with a small red seal. A 42-year-old Chinese businessman in a white shirt stands before it with his back half to camera, looking up at it, and whispers in Mandarin Chinese: "见路不走……可我现在能见到的只有一条死路。" Camera slowly pulls back, he walks out of frame toward the desk; the banner stays in frame. After a pause, a single sharp gunshot is heard off-screen and the desk lamp light flickers. Static, quiet, moody, film grain. No subtitles.
```

## 对比要看什么

| 镜头 | 重点 |
|---|---|
| A | 白色运动卫衣、台阶场景是否准确；中文台词语气 |
| B | 中文口型同步、情绪表演、听筒里的声音 |
| C | 能否正确写出「见路不走」四个字；枪声处理会不会被审核拦下 |
